No Innermost Secrets. Just Truth.

Artisanal coffee roasted in our vintage cast-iron drum roaster "Colossus".

Steampunk Coffee Culture in Cape Town

Truth Coffee Roasting offers extraordinary coffee experiences driven by an uncompromising anti-snobbery philosophy. Located in the heart of Buitenkant Street, we blend mechanical steampunk heritage with world-class coffee craftsmanship.

From custom single-origin imports to sensory-rich espresso blends, every batch is precision-roasted using vintage techniques and cut edge flavor profiling.

Our Story & Steampunk Heritage

Voted one of the best coffee shops in the world, Truth Coffee Roasting turns artisan roasting into a living industrial art form.

The Buitenkant HQ

Designed around our iconic vintage cast-iron roaster 'Colossus', our headquarters features leather, brass, copper, and mechanical clockwork elements built for lovers of raw industrial design.

The Anti-Snobbery Mandate

We reject pretension in favor of pure excellence. Our mission is to roast coffee so good that you will never need or want to add sugar to your cup.
