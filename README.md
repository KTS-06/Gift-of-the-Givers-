# Gift-of-the-Givers

Student number:ST10493438

Name: Gift of the Givers Foundation 
Brief History: Founded in 1992 by Dr. Imtiaz Sooliman following a spiritual instruction in Istanbul, Gift of the Givers has grown into the largest disaster response non-governmental organization of African origin on the continent, delivering over R6 billion in aid across 47 countries over 33 years. 
Mission and Vision: The primary mission is to bring unconditional humanitarian aid, restore hope, and rebuild vulnerable communities irrespective of race, religion, color, class, or political affiliation. 
Target Audience: General corporate and individual financial donors, local and international volunteers, corporate social investment managers, media outlets, and vulnerable communities seeking institutional assistance.

Website Goals and Objectives 

•	Optimize Donor Conversions: Streamline the donation process via local gateways to increase monthly digital funding by 25%. 

•	Enhance Volunteer Recruitment: Create automated onboarding forms to systematically scale up active field operations. 
 
•	Improve Crisis Communication: Launch a low-bandwidth, live-updating crisis news repository for active emergency relief reporting. 
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): 
1. Monthly, total monetary donations are completed via the desktop and mobile interface. 
2. Bounce rate reduction on the primary call-to-action (CTA) donation checkout landing pages. 3. Submission rates for complete volunteer applications through the automated workflow.
